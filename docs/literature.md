# Literature

Papers and references relevant to this research project.

## Papers

- **Schapiro et al. (2026, ICLR 27 preprint)** - *Measuring Combinatorial Creativity in Large Language Models and Humans* (KOMBINE). The empirical companion paper. Defines association, analogy, and blending tasks on entity-relation-entity paths, scores utility/surprise/originality and emergent creativity of invented concepts. Not yet in `literature/` (only shared as an attachment); source is the ICLR preprint PDF.
- **Elmoznino, Jiralerspong, Bengio, Lajoie (2024, arXiv:2410.14817)** - *A Complexity-Based Theory of Compositionality*. Defines representational compositionality C(Z) = K(Z)/K(Z|W) via a Kolmogorov decomposition K(p_w) + K(W|p_w) + K(f) + K(Z|W,f). The semantics f plays the role of KOMBINE's structure mapping M and generic space g. Local copy: `literature/elmoznino_2024_compositionality/` (paper.pdf + paper.txt, extracted with pypdf; figures not extracted).
